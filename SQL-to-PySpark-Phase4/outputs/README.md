# Outputs

This folder contains the output screenshots generated during the execution of the **Phase 4 – Business Pipeline & Analytics** project.

## Files

- **task1_daily_sales.png** – Daily sales summary by sale date.
- **task2_city_revenue.png** – Total revenue generated for each city.
- **task3_top5_customers.png** – Top 5 customers based on total spending.
- **task4_repeat_customers.png** – Customers with more than one order.
- **task5_customer_segmentation.png** – Customer segmentation into **Gold**, **Silver**, and **Bronze** based on total spending.
- **task6_final_report.png** – Final reporting table containing customer name, city, total spend, order count, and segment.
